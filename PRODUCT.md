## Register

brand

The public website presents the studio and guides visitors to training, schedules

## Users

Spanish-speaking people seeking structured training, including beginners,
runners and athletes interested in hybrid training and hybrid competitions. They value coaching,
small groups, the quality of training and belonging to a community. Visitors
explore the studio on phones and desktops before deciding to book a session.

## Product Purpose

Introduce 2707 Altitud as a boutique hybrid and functional training studio in
Zinacantepec. Help people understand the experience, find their training and
reserve with motivation and confidence. Public booking links require sign-in and return the member to the booking
calendar after login or registration. The explicitly labelled member preview
remains a demonstration. Business prices, weekday class times, contact details and cancellation rules were confirmed by the studio on 7 September 2026. Weekend times remain tentative. Official class and package names and validity for the trial, single class and first-five offer are pending. Use descriptive names and never invent missing conditions, operating details, statistics or testimonials.

## Brand Personality

Disciplined, energetic, welcoming. Premium and aspirational while remaining
approachable. Performance Lifestyle combines training, music, design, community
and personal progress. The identity draws on altitude, the Nevado de Toluca,
discipline and evolution.

## Anti-references

Avoid intimidating traditional gym imagery, aggressive sales language,
pretentious luxury, generic fitness templates, excessive visual noise and
branding or photographs from the previous studio. Do not reinterpret the logo.

## Design Principles

1. Make the first training session feel approachable, regardless of experience.
2. Give the training and community more emphasis than decoration.
3. Use the official brand consistently across the website and member app.
4. Make booking actions easy to find, with honest availability information.
5. Keep navigation and reading clear on a phone as well as a desktop.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No additional user-specific accessibility needs have been stated. Preserve the
existing skip links, visible keyboard focus, semantic headings, labelled controls
and reduced-motion support. Avoid relying on color alone to indicate states.
Legibility is especially important with the brand's light body typeface.
